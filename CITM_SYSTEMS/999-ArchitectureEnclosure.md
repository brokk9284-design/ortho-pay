# Architecture Philosophy

The architecture models reality before modelling solutions.

A solution never exists independently.

Every solution has:

• an objective

• a problem

• constraints

• causes

• evidence

• uncertainty

• consequences

• execution requirements

• scientific value

The architecture therefore models intelligence as a continuous transformation of reality into executable knowledge.

Every engine shall have one responsibility.

No duplicated intelligence.

No duplicated state.

No duplicated evidence.

Every resource is versioned.

Every relationship is explicit.

Every uncertainty is measurable.

Every decision is reproducible.
